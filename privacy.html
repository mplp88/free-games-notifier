Free Games Notifier

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Free Games Notifier is an independent Telegram bot that provides notifications and information about free video games available on platforms such as Steam and Epic Games.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is stored when you interact with the bot.

2. Information We Collect

Free Games Notifier is designed to operate with minimal user data.

We only store your Telegram User ID, and only when you explicitly subscribe to notifications using the /subscribe command.

We do not store:

  • Your name or display name.
  • Your Telegram username.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your profile picture.
  • Any other personal profile information.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your Telegram User ID is used exclusively to:

  • Manage your notification subscription.
  • Identify your account when sending free game notifications.
  • Prevent duplicate notifications.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, analytics, or marketing purposes.

4. Data Collection and Storage

You can use the following commands without subscribing or having your information stored:

  • /current – View currently available free games.
  • /next – View upcoming free games.
  • /steam – View free games available on Steam.
  • /epic – View free games available on Epic Games.

Your Telegram User ID is stored only when you use /subscribe.

When you execute /unsubscribe, your ID is removed from the active subscriptions table.

For a limited period, your ID may remain in the notification history table until the current notification rotation is completed. This is necessary to prevent duplicate notifications if you subscribe again during the same rotation.

Once that rotation is completed, the corresponding historical records are automatically removed.

5. Third-Party Services

Free Games Notifier interacts with third-party services to retrieve publicly available information about free games and promotions, including:

  • Steam
  • Epic Games

The bot uses the Telegram Bot API to communicate with users and deliver notifications.

We do not sell, rent, or share stored Telegram User IDs with Steam, Epic Games, or other third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your Telegram User ID is retained in the active subscriptions table while you remain subscribed.

When you unsubscribe, it is removed from that table. Any remaining notification history is retained only until the current rotation is completed, after which it is automatically deleted.

If you wish to request the deletion of any remaining information associated with your Telegram User ID, you can contact the developer directly.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information against unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.

Since the bot stores only the minimum information required to deliver notifications, the amount of retained user data is intentionally limited.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the bot's functionality or data handling practices.

Any updates will be published on this page.
